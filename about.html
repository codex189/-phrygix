About PHRYGIX

Useful privacy tools. Less noise. More control.

Privacy should be practical.

PHRYGIX is a collection of digital utilities designed to help people make more informed choices about what they share online. Our focus is straightforward tools, understandable interfaces, and clear explanations of what each feature can—and cannot—do.

Explore the toolkit

Identity Lab ↗

Create fictional profiles and sample identity data for testing, design, and creative work.

Temporary Email ↗

Use short-lived inboxes for low-stakes situations when the service is configured and available.

Link Inspector ↗

Examine supported redirects and identify common URL tracking parameters.

Secret Vault ↗

Create and share encrypted notes with expiration controls, subject to service availability.

Metadata Cleaner ↗

Review and remove supported image metadata before sharing.

How we approach privacy

We aim to keep tools simple and favor browser-side processing where practical. Some features require server infrastructure and may process or temporarily retain information to function. Individual tool behavior and security protections should be reviewed before relying on them for sensitive material.

Built for real-world use

Whether you are a developer generating test data, someone examining an unfamiliar URL, or a person preparing an image to share, PHRYGIX aims to make common digital tasks easier to understand.

PHRYGIX does not promise complete anonymity, perfect security, or that every tool is available at all times. Check each tool's current status and limitations.
