Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our privacy toolkit.

Find quick answers about the toolkit, how it works, and its limitations.

What is PHRYGIX?

PHRYGIX is a website offering practical tools for fictional test identities, temporary email, link inspection, encrypted notes, and image metadata cleaning.

Are the tools free?

The current toolkit is presented as accessible without a paid plan. Features, usage limits, or pricing may change; check the individual tool page for current availability.

Do I need an account?

Some browser-side tools may be usable without an account. Server-backed features may require additional configuration or service access.

Is Identity Lab for creating real identities?

No. It generates fictional sample data for testing, design, education, and creative purposes. It must not be used for fraud or impersonation.

Can I use Temporary Email for banking or password recovery?

No. Temporary inboxes are not recommended for financial services, important accounts, confidential correspondence, or recovery addresses.

Does Link Inspector guarantee a URL is safe?

No. It can inspect supported redirect patterns and common tracking parameters, but it is not a guarantee against phishing, malware, or other risks.

What does Secret Vault do?

It is designed for sharing encrypted notes through expiring links. Availability depends on server configuration; verify its security and reliability before sharing sensitive information.

Does Metadata Cleaner remove GPS coordinates?

It is designed to remove supported metadata, potentially including location tags. Results depend on the file and export method. Always verify the downloaded file.

Is everything processed locally in my browser?

Not necessarily. Some tools can perform operations in the browser; other functions require a server. Do not assume all inputs stay on your device.

Can PHRYGIX guarantee anonymity?

No. No website can guarantee complete anonymity or absolute security. Consider your browser, network, accounts, and the services you use.

Why is a feature not working?

Some features require backend setup or may be temporarily unavailable. Try again later and review any message shown on the relevant tool page.

Where can I read the usage rules?

Read the Terms & Conditions page for permitted uses, prohibited activities, and tool-specific limitations.

Need to understand a tool? Open its page and review its on-screen instructions and limitations before use.
