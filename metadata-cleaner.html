What's removed?

● YOUR FILES STAY LOCAL

Images are decoded and re-exported through the browser's Canvas API. This tool does not upload images or contact an image-processing API.

Important limitations
Re-encoding may change quality, color profiles, transparency, or animation. This is not forensic sanitization. Identifying content visible in the pixels remains. For sensitive photos, inspect the downloaded file before sharing.

How to use
