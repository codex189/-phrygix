04 / Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

What is PHRYGIX?

PHRYGIX is a collection of browser-based and server-backed utilities for common privacy tasks, including temporary email, fictional test data, link inspection, metadata management and private notes.

Can I use a temporary email address for verification?

A temporary inbox can receive messages sent to its supported domain while the service is available. Some websites block disposable email addresses; do not use them for accounts you need to recover later.

Does Link Inspector guarantee a link is safe?

No. It helps inspect supported URL patterns and tracking parameters, but it is not a comprehensive malware or phishing scanner.

Can removing EXIF metadata make a photo anonymous?

Removing supported metadata may reduce information shared with a photo, but image content and other identifying signals can remain.

Are Identity Lab profiles real people?

No. They are synthetic profiles intended for legitimate testing, design and creative uses, not impersonation or identity verification.
